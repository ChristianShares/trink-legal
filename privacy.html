Privacy Policy – Trink!

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Summary

Trink! does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app contains no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It works entirely offline.

Local data

Custom card decks and settings (such as appearance) are stored exclusively on your device. They never leave your device and are never accessible to us. Deleting the app removes them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We receive no personal data in the process – in particular no payment or account information. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Your rights

Since we process no personal data, there are no data records to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions, contact us at the email address above.

Changes

If the app's functionality changes, this policy will be updated accordingly. The current version is always available on this page.
